Quadratic Tug of War

Two teams race to solve quadratic problems with pinch-and-drop answer blocks. Every correct answer pulls the rope toward your side.

  1. A coin flip starts each round. The winner chooses the topic or lets the other team choose.
  2. Correct answer: pull 12 plus up to 8 speed bonus. Every 3rd answer in a row is a Power Pull ×2.
  3. Wrong answer: the team slips and is frozen for 3 seconds.
  4. Pull the flag to your line to win. When time runs out, the flag's side wins. A tie goes to sudden death.

Each player uses one hand on their team's half of the screen: pinch a block to grab it, open your fingers to drop it. Camera play needs the downloaded file opened in Chrome or Edge.
